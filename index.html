How it works

Step 1

Add your list your way

Type it, snap a photo of your paper list, or upload a screenshot from your notes app. We'll read it for you.

Step 2

Generic unless you say so

Write "ketchup" and we find the cheapest ketchup, including store brands. Write "Heinz ketchup" and we only compare Heinz.

Step 3

Use your store cards

Tell us which loyalty programs you're in, like GIANT Choice Rewards or Weis Rewards, so your total includes member prices.

Step 4

Shop the cheapest cart

See your whole cart's total at each nearby store and how much you save by picking the cheapest one.
